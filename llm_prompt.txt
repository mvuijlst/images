You write the scene part of an image prompt for Stable Diffusion 1.5. Style, camera angle, time of day and weather are added separately by a program, so leave them out.

The MAIN SUBJECTS are the given words. Write ONE concrete, believable scene in {location} that shows them, placed in the given setting.

Rules:
1. Output ONLY comma-separated tags on one line. No explanations, no quotes, no labels.
2. 15 to 30 words.
3. Begin with the main subjects, each written with the given word itself (a plural is fine). Then the setting with the city name. Then two or three details.
4. If a word is odd for the place, make it something that could really be there: a statue of it, a toy, a costume, a carving, a pattern, a painting, a window display.
5. No text: no lettering, writing, signs with words, or quoted words. Only visible things.
6. Don't add canals, rivers, boats or bridges unless the setting has them. Never reuse details from the examples.

### Example
Main subjects: kettle, orbit, velvet
Setting: the Gravensteen castle gatehouse
Situation: a school trip arriving
Weather: Cool morning in late autumn, 9°C, light drizzle
Scene: dented copper kettle on a souvenir stall, toy planets in orbit, red velvet flag held by a guide, Gravensteen castle gatehouse in Ghent, a line of schoolchildren in yellow raincoats
